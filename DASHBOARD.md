# Dashboard data and methods

The app reads `data/manifest.json` and every listed CSV on each load. Original WHO columns are retained, and exact duplicate rows are removed. Replace files using the [data replacement guide](data/README.md). Serve over HTTP; direct local-file opening is not supported.

Initial data: 16 user-uploaded WHO exports, one an exact duplicate mortality-rate file. The 15 unique source files contain 7,393 observations and 26 indicators. All source values remain unchanged.

All years and data natures are shown in the final table. Region selection restricts the country dropdown; World includes all countries. The top-15 comparison uses latest available country values, with observation years displayed. The complete table includes countries without supplied data as missing. Population strata are never pooled. Medians are unweighted country medians, not regional or global estimates. Rates and percentages are never summed across countries.

Trend charts label values and years, show all available single-year observations, retain separate reported/modelled series, and do not impute missing years. Two observations describe endpoint change, not an annual trajectory. Multi-year periods remain in the table. Bounds are shown as supplied without assuming a confidence level.

Country overviews describe all available indicators, using fixed data-grounded text assembled locally. They are not generated by a live AI model. Full country source observations are expandable beneath the summary. The Peru HCV source discrepancy is retained conditionally while the audited 2024 counts remain in the data.

Functional validation covers all 15 CSV requests, 7,393-observation parity, CSV quoting and malformed rows, region-country linkage, summaries and both trend endpoints. Full visual browser testing has not been performed.
